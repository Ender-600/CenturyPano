    Walking and motion

    Walking starts automatically in a generated 3D world when location and direction are available.

    Walking uses the initial compass alignment automatically. GPS movement is approximate and updates as your phone reports its position; individual steps may not be visible.

    Uses the world’s scale when available, or an estimate of 1 world unit per meter. Measure a known distance to refine it.

    Move within a safe, open area. The generated scene has no collision protection, and real obstacles may differ from the historical view. Resetting the start maps your current phone position to the frozen viewpoint.
